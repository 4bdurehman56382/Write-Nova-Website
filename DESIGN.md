# WriteNova visual system

## Theme

Dark, high-contrast content agency surface with a warm coral signal. The product reference informs composition and energy, not copied assets or content.

## Tokens

```css
--ink: #161316;
--brown: #453027;
--coral: #ff6d29;
--beige: #f2e5d4;
--muted: #bababa;
```

## Typography

General Sans is used in 400–700 weights. Headings are compact with a measured negative letterspace (no tighter than -0.04em); paragraphs stay under 70ch.

## Layout

One-page narrative: conversion-first hero, services, proof of fit, process, industries, about, FAQs, project inquiry, footer. The desktop grid uses 12 columns; mobile collapses gracefully with touch-safe controls.

## Motion

Use decisive ease-out movement for the hero, short feedback transitions for controls, and one-time group reveals only where they improve hierarchy. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
