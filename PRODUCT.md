# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business owners, marketing teams, and agency partners who need capable writing support. They arrive assessing whether WriteNova understands their brief, can reflect a brand voice, and will deliver dependable, conversion-minded work.

## Product Purpose

Position WriteNova as a clear, capable content partner and convert qualified visitors into project inquiries. Success is a visitor understanding the service fit quickly, trusting the working process, and completing the inquiry form with useful project details.

## Brand Personality

Assured, considered, and alive. The site should feel like an experienced editorial partner: direct enough to move a project forward, warm enough to invite a conversation, and precise enough to earn trust.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic pens, pencils, stock writer imagery, SaaS metric theatre, all-purpose agency templates, excessive glass effects, and white page surfaces. Do not imitate the supplied website's software products, portraits, or company-specific claims.

## Design Principles

1. Let the writing do the persuasion: make messaging readable, specific, and confident.
2. Use contrast to create momentum: dark restraint with deliberate coral energy.
3. Make a complex service menu easy to scan, then easy to inquire about.
4. Build trust through process clarity rather than unsupported performance claims.
5. Treat motion as pacing and feedback, never as an obstacle to reading or action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA intent: strong contrast, semantic landmarks, keyboard support, visible focus, 44 px touch targets, labels rather than placeholder-only fields, useful validation messages, and a reduced-motion alternative.
